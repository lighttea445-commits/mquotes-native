# brand.md — Quotable

## Brand Details

**Name:** Quotable
**Category:** Lifestyle / Self-improvement
**Platform:** Android

**What it is:** A premium minimalist motivation app. Every screen is intentional — dark themes, serif typography, gold accents. It feels elevated and focused, not cluttered or loud.

**Tone:** Calm. Refined. Unhurried. The app doesn't push — it offers. Language is minimal and warm, never motivational-poster loud.

**Visual identity:** Near-black backgrounds, warm cream text, gold as the single accent. Playfair Display for quotes (literary, weighted), Inter for UI (clean, unobtrusive). 18 themes let users make it theirs without breaking the aesthetic.

**Core promise:** One great quote a day can shift how you see a moment. Quotable makes that easy, beautiful, and personal.

---

## Ideal Customer Profile

**Age:** 22–40
**Mindset:** Reflective, growth-oriented, aesthetically aware
**Behavior:** Reads, journals, or meditates — or wants to. Curates their phone's home screen. Pays for apps that earn their place.

**They follow:** authors, philosophers, slow-living creators, book accounts on Instagram/TikTok
**They use:** Notion, Day One, Headspace, Kindle
**They value:** depth over volume, calm over stimulation, beauty in the everyday

---

## Decision Factors

- **Design quality** — If the app looks cheap, they're out immediately. First impression is everything.
- **Quote quality** — Generic motivational fluff is a dealbreaker. Wisdom, literature, philosophy land.
- **Personalization** — Themes, categories, mixes. It needs to feel like theirs.
- **No noise** — No gamification badges, no pushy notifications, no dark patterns. Trust is slow to build, fast to lose.
- **Widget** — Home screen presence matters. A beautiful widget is a daily touchpoint that doubles as word-of-mouth.

---

## Buying Triggers

- **The widget moment** — They see someone's home screen with a Quotable widget and want it.
- **A quote that lands** — One line stops them mid-swipe. They screenshot it. They share it. Then they wonder what else is in here.
- **The free limit** — Theme lock or history cutoff surfaces at exactly the right moment — after they're already invested.
- **Streak psychology** — A 7-day streak feels worth protecting. The upgrade removes the ceiling on what the streak can mean.
- **Gift / recommendation** — Shared via a story or direct message. Low friction to download when someone they trust sent it.
